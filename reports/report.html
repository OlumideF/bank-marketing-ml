5. Data Leakage Documentation

What is duration? Call length in seconds, recorded only after a call ends.

Why is it leaky? You cannot know duration before deciding to dial a customer. Using it inflates metrics and creates a model that is impossible to deploy for prioritization.

What the AUC gap reveals: v1 AUC 0.955 vs v2 AUC 0.810 (Δ = 0.144). Much of v1's apparent power comes from post-call information, not pre-call signals. v2 still achieves strong ranking (AUC 0.810) with only information available at campaign time.
